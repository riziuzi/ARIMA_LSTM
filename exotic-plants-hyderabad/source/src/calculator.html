4 · Your loan and salary

Banks usually allow all your EMIs together to take 40–50% of your take-home pay. The safest plan is an EMI you can pay from salary alone, even if the farm earns nothing for two years.

5 · Government support

Subsidies arrive after you build, and only if your application is sanctioned. Switch them off to see the farm without help.
